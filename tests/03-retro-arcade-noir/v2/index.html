OUR LITTLE STORY

Some shops sell games.
We grow them.

Daydream Cartridge started as a flower stall with one busted cabinet propped beside the tulips. Thirty-odd years later it's a sunlit maze of CRTs and hanging baskets — every screen plugged in, every one of them playable.

We restore, we trade, we ship worldwide wrapped in pastel tissue with a pressed flower inside. Bring your old collection by any afternoon — the tea's on us.
