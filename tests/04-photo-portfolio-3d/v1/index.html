About

I photograph the moment just before the light decides.

For fourteen years I have followed weather across northern coasts, high deserts and empty cities at dawn. I work slowly, mostly on medium format, and often return to the same place for years.

My work has appeared in Kinfolk, Cereal and National Geographic Traveller, and is held in private collections in eleven countries. I accept a few commissions each season.
