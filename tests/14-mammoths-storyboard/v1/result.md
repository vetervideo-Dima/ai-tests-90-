# "The Last Herd" — 30-second nature-documentary sequence

**Source prompt (Sora demo):** *Several giant wooly mammoths approach treading through a snowy meadow, their long wooly fur lightly blows in the wind as they walk, snow covered trees and dramatic snow capped mountains in the distance, mid afternoon light with wispy clouds and a sun high in the distance creates a warm glow, the low camera view is stunning capturing the large furry mammal with beautiful photography, depth of field.*

**Format:** 16:9, 24 fps, 30.0 s total (720 frames). Look: large-format nature doc (think *Planet Earth* / *Prehistoric Planet*), naturalistic grade, warm highlights / cool shadows.

---

## Global continuity bible

| Element | Locked value |
|---|---|
| **Sun** | Mid-afternoon, ~35° elevation, positioned **camera-left, slightly behind the herd** (key comes from frame-left / upper-left in every shot facing the herd). Herd walks **left → right** across the screen axis, so sun is always on their back-left flank, producing a warm rim on fur edges and shadows falling **toward camera-right and slightly toward lens**. |
| **Color temperature** | Key ~4800 K (warm gold), shadows/sky fill ~7500 K (pale blue). Never sunset orange. |
| **Fur color** | Dark chestnut-brown base (#4A2E1F), sun-bleached ginger-auburn tips (#B5713A) on shoulders and back; longer dark guard hairs hanging to knees; no grey, no black. Calf: lighter, fluffier, honey-brown. |
| **Tusks** | Ivory with yellowed base, long curved inward; lead matriarch has one chipped tusk tip (right tusk) — use as identity marker. |
| **Herd** | 4 adults + 1 calf. Matriarch leads, calf walks on her far side (screen-right) in wide shots. |
| **Snow on ground** | Fresh powder ~30–40 cm deep, legs sink to mid-shin; trail of trampled snow behind herd. Tufts of dry golden grass poke through in the foreground. |
| **Falling snow / air** | **Light**, sparse windblown flakes and sun-lit ice crystals (diamond dust), never a snowstorm. Density constant through shots 1–7; slightly increases only in shot 8 as wind picks up. |
| **Wind** | Light breeze from screen-left to screen-right — fur and breath vapor drift **right**. |
| **Background** | Snow-laden spruce forest mid-ground, jagged snow-capped range behind, wispy cirrus clouds. Peak with a distinctive double summit always sits right of center when visible. |
| **Breath** | Visible warm breath vapor from trunks every 2–3 s. |

## Shot list

**Running total in brackets.**

### Shot 1 — Establishing landscape — 5.0 s [0:00–0:05]
- **Framing:** Extreme wide shot (EWS). Mammoths are tiny dark shapes in the lower third; mountains dominate.
- **Lens:** 24 mm, deep focus (f/11).
- **Camera move:** Slow aerial drone push-in and gentle descent, ~2 m/s.
- **Image-to-video prompt:** *A vast snowy meadow under jagged snow-capped mountains in warm mid-afternoon sun, a small herd of woolly mammoths walking left to right as dark silhouettes in the distance, wispy clouds, slow aerial push-in descending toward them, photorealistic nature documentary.*

### Shot 2 — Low ground hero — 4.0 s [0:05–0:09]
- **Framing:** Wide, ultra-low angle from snow level; the herd approaches toward camera diagonally, lead matriarch filling the right half of frame by the end.
- **Lens:** 35 mm, f/2.8, foreground snow and grass tufts out of focus.
- **Camera move:** Static on a ground slider, very slight push forward.
- **Image-to-video prompt:** *Low camera at snow level as giant woolly mammoths tread toward the lens through deep powder, long chestnut fur swaying in the breeze, snow-covered spruce and mountains behind, warm backlight rimming their fur, shallow depth of field.*

### Shot 3 — Feet in snow — 3.0 s [0:09–0:12]
- **Framing:** Close-up on a massive foot and lower leg plunging into snow.
- **Lens:** 85 mm macro-style, f/2.
- **Camera move:** Locked off; slow motion (48 fps conformed to 24).
- **Image-to-video prompt:** *Close-up slow motion of a woolly mammoth's huge shaggy foot pressing deep into fresh powder snow, ice crystals bursting up and glittering in warm sunlight, long dark hair brushing the snow.*

### Shot 4 — Tracking side profile — 5.0 s [0:12–0:17]
- **Framing:** Medium-wide profile of matriarch walking left to right, calf partially hidden behind her legs.
- **Lens:** 200 mm telephoto, f/4, compressed background mountains.
- **Camera move:** Lateral tracking shot matching walking speed (vehicle/cable-cam).
- **Image-to-video prompt:** *Telephoto tracking shot of a woolly mammoth matriarch walking left to right in profile through a snowy meadow, a fluffy calf beside her, wind lifting ginger fur tips, mountains compressed and softly blurred behind, warm afternoon light from the left.*

### Shot 5 — Eye detail — 3.0 s [0:17–0:20]
- **Framing:** Extreme close-up of matriarch's eye and lashes, frost on fur around it.
- **Lens:** 300 mm, f/5.6.
- **Camera move:** Very slow push-in.
- **Image-to-video prompt:** *Extreme close-up of a woolly mammoth's amber eye with frost-tipped lashes, she blinks slowly, fine snow crystals on surrounding brown fur glowing in warm sunlight, breath vapor drifting past.*

### Shot 6 — Calf moment — 3.0 s [0:20–0:23]
- **Framing:** Medium shot of the calf trotting to catch up, tossing its trunk.
- **Lens:** 135 mm, f/2.8.
- **Camera move:** Handheld-style follow pan right.
- **Image-to-video prompt:** *A fluffy honey-brown woolly mammoth calf trots through snow to catch up with the herd, playfully swinging its small trunk and kicking up powder, warm side light, shallow depth of field, nature documentary.*

### Shot 7 — Herd passes the lens — 4.0 s [0:23–0:27]
- **Framing:** Medium-wide from low angle; mammoths pass very close, one fills frame like a wall of fur (natural wipe).
- **Lens:** 28 mm, f/4.
- **Camera move:** Static, slight upward tilt as the nearest mammoth passes.
- **Image-to-video prompt:** *Low angle as enormous woolly mammoths walk past the camera very close, towering over the lens, long shaggy fur sweeping across the frame, tusks catching golden sunlight, snowy mountains glimpsed between them.*

### Shot 8 — Departure — 3.0 s [0:27–0:30]
- **Framing:** Wide from behind; herd walks away toward the mountains, trampled trail leading to them.
- **Lens:** 50 mm, f/8.
- **Camera move:** Slow crane up and pull back.
- **Image-to-video prompt:** *From behind, a herd of woolly mammoths walks away across a vast snowy meadow toward towering snow-capped mountains, a trampled trail behind them, light snow beginning to fall, warm afternoon sun, slow crane up.*

**Total: 5 + 4 + 3 + 5 + 3 + 3 + 4 + 3 = 30.0 s**

| **M3 hit** | 0:09 | Hard sync: a single deep drum hit on the foot impact in shot 3, music drops to near silence for 1 s. |

| **M6 climax** | 0:23 | Full orchestra swell as herd passes the lens; peaks at 0:26. |
