# "The Last Herd" — 30-second nature-documentary sequence

**Source prompt (Sora demo):** *Several giant wooly mammoths approach treading through a snowy meadow, their long wooly fur lightly blows in the wind as they walk, snow covered trees and dramatic snow capped mountains in the distance, mid afternoon light with wispy clouds and a sun high in the distance creates a warm glow, the low camera view is stunning capturing the large furry mammal with beautiful photography, depth of field.*

**Delivery:** 16:9, 3840×2160, 24 fps, **30.0 s = 720 frames**. Look: large-format wildlife documentary, naturalistic grade, warm highlights / cool blue shadows, fine film grain, no lens flares except shot 7.

**Arc:** Scale (1) → Arrival (2–3) → Family (4–6) → Power (7) → Departure (8).

## Continuity bible (locked across all shots)

**Geography & screen direction.** The herd travels **screen-left → screen-right** in every shot (the 180° line runs along their path; the camera always stays on the same, near side). Because they face screen-right, the camera always sees their **right flank**. Mountains sit **behind** the herd; the hero peak (distinctive **double summit**) is always **right of centre** when visible.

**Light direction.** Mid-afternoon sun at **~35° elevation**, **upper screen-left and behind the herd** (back-left of the animals). Result in every shot:
- warm **rim/edge light** on the top line of backs, heads and fur fringes;
- the camera-facing flank in soft shade, filled by cool **snow bounce from below**;
- shadows fall **toward screen-right and slightly toward the lens**, medium length (about 1.4× object height — not sunset-long).
Key ~4800 K warm gold; shadow/sky fill ~7500 K pale blue. Never orange sunset, never overcast.

**Fur color.**
- Adults: dark chestnut-brown base (**#4A2E1F**), sun-bleached ginger-auburn tips (**#B5713A**) on shoulders, hump and crown; long dark guard hairs hanging to the knees. No grey, no black, no reddish-orange.
- Calf: lighter, fluffier, honey-brown (**#9C6B3E**), short fringe, no visible tusks.
- Frost: a fine white rime on muzzle hair and eyelashes only.

**Identity markers.** Herd = **4 adults + 1 calf**. Matriarch leads, is the largest, and has a **chipped tip on her right tusk** (the camera-facing tusk, so it reads in shots 2, 4, 7). The calf walks on the matriarch's **near side, slightly behind her front legs** (partly hidden by them in shot 4).

**Snow on the ground.** Fresh powder 30–40 cm deep; legs sink to mid-shin; a churned trench trail runs behind the herd. Dry golden grass tufts poke through in foregrounds. Snow-laden spruce in the mid-ground.

**Falling snow / air density.** Level **1 (light)** in shots 1–7: sparse wind-blown flakes plus sunlit **diamond-dust** ice crystals, about 1–2 visible flakes per 10% of frame. Shot 8 goes to **level 2 (light-moderate)**, motivated by the wind picking up; this is the only change. Never a blizzard, never fog.

**Wind & breath.** Light breeze blowing **screen-left → screen-right**; fur fringes, flakes and breath vapor all drift **right**. Each adult exhales a visible warm vapor puff every 2–3 s.

**Sky.** Pale blue with wispy cirrus, upper-right of frame.

**Shared style suffix (add to every I2V prompt):** *photorealistic wildlife documentary, 4K, natural motion, warm mid-afternoon backlight from upper left, wind blowing left to right, light diamond-dust snow in the air.*
**Negative prompt (all shots):** *cartoon, CGI look, elephants, grey skin, bare skin, extra legs, extra tusks, blizzard, fog, sunset, orange sky, lens flare (except shot 7), text, watermark, humans.*

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — "The Range" — 4.0 s [00:00–00:04]
- **Framing:** Extreme wide (EWS). Mammoths are small dark shapes on the lower third and their trail is a thin line; the mountains fill the top two thirds, with the double summit right of centre.
- **Lens:** 24 mm, f/11, deep focus.
- **Camera move:** Drone push-in with slow descent from ~80 m to ~50 m, constant speed, no rotation.
- **Start frame:** a still of the snowy valley with the herd already in place at lower-left-centre.
- **I2V prompt:** *A slow aerial push-in descends over a vast sunlit snowy meadow toward a small herd of dark-brown woolly mammoths walking left to right beneath jagged snow-capped mountains with a double-summit peak and wispy cirrus clouds.*
- **Continuity:** Shadows to screen-right; herd's trail extends back to frame-left.

### Shot 2 — "Arrival" — 4.0 s [00:04–00:08]
- **Framing:** Wide, lens 15 cm above the snow. The herd approaches on a diagonal from mid-left toward foreground-right. By the end the matriarch fills the right half of the frame.
- **Lens:** 35 mm, f/2.8. Foreground grass tufts and snow crust are soft, the matriarch is sharp.
- **Camera move:** Slow push-in on a ground slider (0.3 m over the shot).
- **Start frame:** low snow-level view, mammoths in mid-ground, matriarch's chipped right tusk readable.
- **I2V prompt:** *From a camera at snow level, giant woolly mammoths tread toward the lens through deep powder, their long chestnut fur with ginger tips swaying in the breeze and glowing with warm rim light, as the camera slowly pushes in with shallow depth of field.*
- **Continuity:** Travel vector still has a clear left→right component. Cut on the matriarch's front-right foot hitting the snow.

### Shot 3 — "Weight" — 3.0 s [00:08–00:11]
- **Framing:** Close-up of the matriarch's **front-right** foot and lower leg (the camera-facing side) plunging into snow.
- **Lens:** 100 mm, f/2.8, focus on the snow surface.
- **Camera move:** Locked off, at snow level. Shot at 48 fps, played at 24 (2× slow motion).
- **I2V prompt:** *In slow motion, a woolly mammoth's huge shaggy foot presses deep into fresh powder snow, throwing up a burst of glittering ice crystals that drift to the right in warm sunlight, while long dark guard hairs brush the snow.*
- **Continuity:** Kicked-up powder is local and does not raise the falling-snow level. Light is a warm side/back edge from frame-left.

### Shot 4 — "The Matriarch" — 5.0 s [00:11–00:16]
- **Framing:** Medium-wide right-side profile of the matriarch walking left → right. The calf is on the near side, half-hidden behind her front legs. Lead room on screen-right.
- **Lens:** 200 mm, f/4. The compressed mountains behind are a soft wash.
- **Camera move:** Lateral tracking at walking pace (≈1.2 m/s) from a tracking vehicle, keeping her centred.
- **I2V prompt:** *In a telephoto tracking shot, a woolly mammoth matriarch walks left to right in profile through a snowy meadow with a fluffy honey-brown calf at her legs, the wind lifting the ginger tips of her chestnut fur, while warm backlight from upper left outlines her hump against softly blurred snowy mountains.*
- **Continuity:** Rim light on the hump and the top of the head, flank in cool shade. Distant trumpet SFX at 00:14.

### Shot 5 — "Memory" — 3.0 s [00:16–00:19]
- **Framing:** Extreme close-up of the matriarch's **right eye**, the lashes and the frosted fur around it.
- **Lens:** 400 mm, f/5.6.
- **Camera move:** Imperceptible push-in (≈3% scale over the shot).
- **I2V prompt:** *In an extreme close-up, the amber eye of a woolly mammoth with frost-tipped lashes blinks slowly while fine ice crystals sparkle on her dark-brown fur and a wisp of warm breath vapor drifts across the frame to the right.*
- **Continuity:** The catchlight sits at upper-left in the eye, which matches the sun. Snow bounce fills the lower lid. Cut on the blink.

### Shot 6 — "The Calf" — 3.0 s [00:19–00:22]
- **Framing:** Medium. The calf trots left → right to catch up, swinging its trunk. Adult legs pass out of focus in the foreground.
- **Lens:** 135 mm, f/2.8.
- **Camera move:** Handheld-feel follow pan right, with slight organic float.
- **I2V prompt:** *As the camera pans right to follow it, a fluffy honey-brown woolly mammoth calf trots through deep snow to catch up with the herd, playfully swinging its small trunk and kicking up sparkling powder in warm afternoon backlight.*
- **Continuity:** The calf has no tusks and is lighter than the adults. It exits frame-right, which motivates the next shot.

### Shot 7 — "Giants" — 4.0 s [00:22–00:26]
- **Framing:** Low medium-wide. Adults pass 2–3 m from the lens and tower over it. In the last ~10 frames the nearest mammoth's flank fills the frame as a wall of fur (a natural wipe).
- **Lens:** 28 mm, f/4.
- **Camera move:** Locked-off base, slow tilt up (~10°) as the nearest mammoth passes.
- **I2V prompt:** *From a low angle, enormous woolly mammoths walk left to right past the camera so close that they tower over it, the sun flaring briefly between their bodies and catching a chipped ivory tusk, until the nearest animal's long shaggy fur sweeps across and fills the frame.*
- **Continuity:** The only shot that allows a sun flare (upper-left, between animals). Peak of the music.

### Shot 8 — "Onward" — 4.0 s [00:26–00:30]
- **Framing:** Wide, from behind-left of the herd (still on the near side of the line). The herd walks away diagonally toward the upper-right into depth, following the trench trail toward the double-summit peak, which sits right of centre.
- **Lens:** 50 mm, f/8.
- **Camera move:** Crane up ~4 m while pulling back slowly. The final 12 frames fade to a pale snow-white.
- **I2V prompt:** *As the camera cranes up and slowly pulls back, a herd of woolly mammoths walks away diagonally toward the upper right across a vast snowy meadow, leaving a trampled trail toward towering snow-capped mountains while a little more snow drifts down in the warm afternoon light.*
- **Continuity:** The sun stays upper screen-left, so the backs and rumps glow ginger. Falling snow rises to **level 2**, the only density change. Screen direction is preserved: the herd moves away to the right, not toward the left.

## Music cues

Score: low strings, French horn, felt piano, soft orchestral percussion. Key D minor resolving to D major. ~60 BPM, with the beat locked to footfalls (one footfall ≈ 1 beat).

| Cue | In | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **M1 "Range"** | 00:00 | Sustained low string pad (D/A) + airy wind bed. A solo horn breathes in at 00:02. |
| **M2 "Pulse"** | 00:04 | Low drum pulse on every mammoth footfall; cellos enter with an ostinato. |
| **M3 "Weight hit"** | 00:08.5 | A single sub-heavy drum hit on the foot impact in shot 3, then the music drops to near-silence (only high strings) until 00:11. |
| **M4 "Theme"** | 00:11 | Main theme on solo horn, strings swelling under the tracking shot. |
| **M5 "Memory"** | 00:16 | Strings hold one sustained chord and the movement stops for the eye close-up. |
| **M6 "Calf"** | 00:19 | Felt-piano / celesta motif, lighter rhythm, cellos out. |
| **M7 "Giants"** | 00:22 | Full orchestra crescendo. **Peak at 00:25.5**, synced to the fur wipe. |
| **M8 "Onward"** | 00:26 | Resolves to D major, the brass falls away, a single horn note plus wind rings out past 00:30 (tail in the fade). |

**SFX by shot:** 1 wind, distant crunch · 2 snow crunch, breath snorts · 3 deep footfall thump + crystal tinkle · 4 rhythmic crunch, distant trumpet call at 00:14 · 5 slow breath, near silence · 6 quick light crunches, a calf squeak · 7 close heavy footfalls, fur swish across the mic at the wipe · 8 wind rising, fading crunch.
