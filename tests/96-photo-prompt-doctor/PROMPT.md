```
Here are three photo prompts written for image models:
(1) "A business portrait of a 38-year-old female executive with European features: shoulder-length straight dark hair, wearing a smart black jacket with a thin white stripe and small gold stud earrings. She looks calmly confident into the camera. Studio lighting: a large softbox to the left and a narrow rim light on the right to highlight facial contours. Neutral dark gray seamless background. Shot with an 85mm lens at f/2.0 for shallow depth of field (focus on eyes, slight blur on ears/shoulders). Skin is highly detailed (visible pores, minimal smoothing)."
(2) "A 32-year-old fair-skinned man with a three-day red stubble and slightly tousled hair, wearing a white linen shirt with rolled-up sleeves. He is seated by a large window, looking thoughtfully just off camera. Morning diffuse light enters from the left, casting soft shadows on the right side of his face. In the background, a café interior is visible out of focus (warm wood tones). Shot on 50mm at f/1.8. Cinematic color grading: cool blue in shadows, warm creamy highlights."
(3) "A pair of clean white low-top sneakers on a wet dark asphalt at night. Shot from ground level at a 10° upward angle, emphasizing the sole and silhouette. The wet pavement shows blurred reflections of city lights. Background: a defocused city street at night – warm orange street lamps on the right, cool blue shop window light on the left, large soft bokeh. Backlighting from a street lamp behind-left creates a glowing rim along the top edge of the sole. Shot on 35mm f/1.4."
For each prompt: give a short critique (what is strong, what is ambiguous or will likely fail), then rewrite it separately for Nano Banana Pro, Midjourney v7, Flux and GPT Image, using each model's preferred structure and parameters, with a negative-cue line where the model supports it. Finish with a one-paragraph checklist of the 7 most important rules for photoreal prompts.
```
